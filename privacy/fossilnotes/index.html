《标准化石笔记》（"本应用"）是一款由 JI GUO（郭际）开发的离线化石学习应用，用于浏览化石数据、查看三维模型与阅读参考文档。本政策说明本应用所处理的数据。

Index Fossil Notes ("the app") is an offline fossil-study app developed by JI GUO, for browsing fossil data, viewing 3D models, and reading reference documents. This policy explains what data the app handles.

数据收集：无 / Data collection: none

本应用不收集、传输、共享或出售任何个人数据。应用内不含任何分析工具、广告、追踪器，且不与任何服务器建立连接。

Index Fossil Notes does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and does not connect to any server.

应用内容 / In-app content

本应用的所有内容——化石数据、三维模型（GLB 格式）与参考文档——均随应用一同打包，仅在本机展示，绝不会上传或发送至任何外部位置。本应用不生成、不存储任何用户个人内容，亦不提供账户系统。

All in-app content — fossil data, 3D models (GLB), and reference documents — is bundled with the app and displayed locally only. It is never uploaded or sent to any external location. The app does not generate or store any user personal content, and provides no account system.
