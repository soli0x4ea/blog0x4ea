Privacy Policy — ModelLens

Last updated: 2026-08-27

ModelLens ("the app") is an offline 3D model viewer developed by JI GUO. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data collection: none

ModelLens does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and does not connect to any server.

Files you open

Models you open are read from your device storage only. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Library entries are stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to issue@0x4ea.com.
